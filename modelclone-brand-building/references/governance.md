# Governance

Use the active user locale, owned brand facts and manual/autonomous gates in the main skill. Do not replace existing ad production quality rules.

## brand-guidelines

Document logo clearance, color values, typography, imagery, voice and application examples. Separate existing standards from proposed changes.

## brand-audit

Compare actual assets across channels against the approved standards; record evidence, inconsistency, consequence and scoped correction.

## brand-measurement

Specify baseline, metric definition, collection source, cadence and decision threshold. Distinguish awareness proxies from measured causal lift.

## rebranding

Identify what must change and what equity must remain; plan asset migration and review gates without automatically replacing live assets.

## Evidence-led audit

Select actual touchpoints relevant to the request: product page, social assets, packaging, app screens or sales materials. Compare them with current approved positioning, voice and identity. Record each finding as source/asset, observed inconsistency, audience consequence, severity and minimal correction. Separate a taste preference from a violation of an agreed standard. An asset not inspected is outside the audit, not implicitly passed.

Review positioning clarity, proof, message hierarchy, voice, logo use, color/type system and cross-channel consistency. Prioritize errors that mislead or prevent comprehension before cosmetic variation. A good audit returns a short correction backlog with evidence and acceptance criteria, not a blanket recommendation to rebrand.

## Standards and change control

A guidelines document should identify approved logo variants, minimum viable size, clearance, forbidden distortion, palette roles, typography hierarchy, image direction, voice examples and channel applications. Provide correct/incorrect examples using owned assets when possible. Distinguish an existing rule from a new proposal and record which version an exported asset used. Do not create fictitious official brand standards from a mood reference.

For a refresh or rebrand, first diagnose the trigger: strategic change, audience confusion, inconsistency or outdated execution. Decide what equity to preserve before naming or identity exploration. Stage changes through direction, approved assets, migration inventory and launch readiness; identify old files or touchpoints that would remain contradictory. Never replace live assets merely because a draft direction has been generated.

## Measurement plan

Match metrics to the decision: awareness, consideration, preference, consistency or commercial contribution. For each, define source, baseline, observation window, population, calculation and review cadence. Distinguish survey measures, search/share-of-voice proxies and revenue outcomes; correlation between a campaign and sales is not proof of causal brand lift. Unknown values stay unavailable, not zero.

Report measured change with sample and comparability limits. If a test is proposed, state the hypothesis, comparison, minimum useful evidence and the action each outcome would support. The deliverable is a traceable audit or measurement specification; implementing trackers, surveys, account settings or recurring jobs is a separate authorized task.
