# Growth

Use the active user locale, owned brand facts and manual/autonomous gates in the main skill. Do not replace existing ad production quality rules.

## brand-launch

Prepare audience, narrative, deliverables, sequencing and measurable objectives. Publishing and customer communications require separate authorization.

## brand-partnerships

Evaluate audience overlap, brand fit, value exchange, responsibilities and rights; draft proposals without contacting partners.

## b2b-brand-marketing

Address the buyer committee, buying risk, procurement evidence and long-cycle touchpoints; tailor proof to each role.

## d2c-marketing

Connect acquisition promise to product demonstration, purchase friction and repeat-use reasons. Ground retention and unit-economics advice in supplied numbers.

## ugc-strategy

Define a believable situation, useful demonstration, creator fit and reuse rights. Synthetic presenters must not impersonate independent satisfied customers.

## influencer-marketing

Prepare creator criteria, briefing, deliverables, rights and measurement. Outreach and contracting are separate actions requiring authorization.

## email-marketing

Draft segment-specific messages, lifecycle sequence and testing hypotheses. Do not send messages, modify lists or claim deliverability evidence without access.

## google-ads

Draft campaign hypotheses, intent groups, creative and measurement needs. Verify current platform constraints before implementation; no automatic campaign spend.

## meta-ads

Draft audience/creative hypotheses, placement variants and test cells. Keep creative production separate from account changes, targeting and placement spend.

## whatsapp-marketing

Draft consent-aware conversational journeys and useful messages. Do not imply an installed WhatsApp integration or send messages.

## aso

Draft store positioning, search-intent terms and screenshot narrative. Verify current store limits and use actual analytics for optimization claims.

## Choose the channel from the buying situation

Start with objective, audience, offer, current baseline and available resources. A channel is justified by where the buyer discovers, evaluates or reorders the product, not by its popularity. Separate acquisition, conversion and retention work. Deliver a compact plan with target segment, message, asset, distribution assumption, measurement and stop/continue criterion. Do not fabricate CAC, conversion rate or return on ad spend when no usable data exists.

For B2B, map economic buyer, user and technical/procurement reviewers to the evidence each needs. Thought leadership serves discovery; implementation detail and credible customer evidence reduce evaluation risk. For D2C, connect the acquisition promise to the product page and demonstration; identify purchase friction and a legitimate repeat-use reason. Subscription advice needs actual repeat behavior and terms, not an assumption that recurring billing improves value.

## Creative and distribution briefs

UGC/influencer briefs specify creator fit, real use situation, product facts, required demonstration, exclusions, deliverables and rights. Compare smaller specialized creators against broad reach by audience relevance and observed engagement quality; follower count alone is insufficient. Paid-ad reuse, term, territory and editing rights are distinct from a creator merely posting. Synthetic presenter demos cannot be presented as an independently earned customer testimonial.

Search-ad planning groups genuine purchase intents and matching landing-page promises. Separate discovery queries from high-intent queries; propose negative themes and tests only with context. Social-ad planning compares meaningful creative hypotheses such as problem recognition, physical demonstration or proof-led explanation while keeping other test variables controlled. Platform character limits, targeting options, bidding features and policy constraints require current verification before implementation. Neither brief creates or funds campaigns.

Email and WhatsApp plans identify consent state, lifecycle trigger, useful message and frequency. Distinguish transactional information from promotional copy; do not imply an installed automation. Store-listing work aligns title/subtitle/search terms and screenshot narrative to the app's actual capability, then proposes tests against available store analytics. Verify current store rules before delivery.

## Launches and partnerships

A launch sequence includes internal readiness, asset checks, audience-specific announcement and a measurable follow-up. Co-branding briefs explain shared audience value, contributions, approval responsibilities and usage rights before visuals are combined. End with dependencies and draft assets, not claims that outreach, publication, campaign activation or partner approval occurred.
