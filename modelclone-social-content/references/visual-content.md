## Select a representation that serves the content

Use structured typography/layout for ordered steps, comparisons, frameworks or data; use photographic/generated imagery when the subject, setting or demonstration carries meaning. An infographic should explain relationships, not decorate a block of text. Whiteboard styling is an optional teaching treatment, not a universal brand direction. Follow the user's dimensions and brand first; verify placement requirements when exporting for a platform.

Create a content inventory before layout: exact headline, all required facts/items, labels/units, approved attribution and CTA. Distil rather than paste the entire post, but do not silently drop required items to meet a word target. Split a dense brief across slides when readability would otherwise fail. Choose hierarchy by importance, consistent margins, limited emphasis and sufficient contrast. Use real supplied logos; generated typography cannot be trusted without inspection.

## Carousel and infographic planning

Plan a carousel as an opening promise, ordered development and a relevant conclusion/CTA. Give each slide a job and a specific headline; keep recurring visual anchors, typography and numbering consistent. Check that individual slides make sense without misleading excerpts and that the sequence delivers the cover promise. Do not call a multi-slide set complete after checking only the cover.

For infographics, match visual form to the relationship: sequence for a process, aligned columns for a comparison, a labeled quantitative scale for measured data. Preserve units, denominators and uncertainty. Avoid pictograms or area scaling that imply numbers not present in the source. If the asset needs precise text, prefer available deterministic composition for lettering while treating generated imagery as the background/component.

## Thumbnails, profiles and visual QA

A thumbnail needs one readable focal point linked to the real video, with any presenter identity held to owned references. Evaluate at roughly 320px width; elaborate detail that disappears there has no communicative value. Profile work should align avatar/banner, positioning and bio, but generating a suggestion does not change the account.

Inspect the actual exported image at native size and about 360px feed width. Check every required text item, spelling, contrast, clipping, font appearance, brand colors, logo proportions, product label and reference fidelity. For product ads also inspect physical scale, plausible material and genuine liquid color. Record defects, fix the affected asset and reinspect. Return the real state—draft prompt, render pending, generated image or inspected export—and only file formats that actually exist. Editable HTML/SVG and raster PNG are different deliverables; never imply one from the other.
